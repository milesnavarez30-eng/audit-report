Rows are sorted internally into Ground Floor, 1st Floor, 2nd Floor, and Review Needed, while the output keeps the original seven-column maintenance-sheet header. After sorting, the result appears directly below in a Google Sheets-style workspace. Edit cells, highlight rows, cut/copy them, or send selected rows directly to Maintenance Report. Ambiguous campaigns that exist on multiple floors are not guessed.
