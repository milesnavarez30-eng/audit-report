Maintenance AI Sorter

Paste maintenance rows, auto-sort them, then work directly inside the editable sheet workspace.

Paste your maintenance report from Google Sheets or a copied table. The header row is optional. The sorter uses your supplied Site A 1st Floor and 2nd Floor Team Leader + campaign references, combines them with Station No. patterns, and learns from the current report. If a new Team Leader name appears that is not in the seat plan, it will automatically use the campaign or explicit floor/station to place the row when the evidence is clear. Rows are sorted internally into Ground Floor, 1st Floor, 2nd Floor, and Review Needed, while the output keeps the original seven-column maintenance-sheet header. After sorting, the result appears directly below in a Google Sheets-style workspace. Edit cells, highlight rows, cut/copy them, or send selected rows directly to Maintenance Report. Ambiguous campaigns that exist on multiple floors are not guessed.
Header optional — you can paste the data rows directly in the normal order: TIMESTAMP → DATE → TL → ACCOUNT → SITE → STATION NO. → STATION ISSUE.
